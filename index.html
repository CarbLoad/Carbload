Set up your log

A few basics let CarbLoad set sensible starting targets — sex affects reference ranges like iron, sport shapes your macro targets, and height (optional) gives you a properly personalized starting estimate on day one instead of a generic guess. Everything here can be changed later on the Log tab.

Quick heads-up: this is sports-nutrition guidance, not medical advice — a sports dietitian is still worth it for anything personal.
Also, the numbers may look big. This app exists to fight athlete underfueling, not shrink your plate — give it a fair shot before assuming it's wrong.

Just a starting point for your first few days — once you've logged enough real training, CarbLoad quietly switches to using your actual volume instead.

Your data is saved to your account and synced to Supabase, so it follows you across devices — it isn't stuck on just this browser anymore. The Whoop numbers below are still typed in by hand, not pulled live; a real version would sync them automatically the way we scoped out for the actual app.
