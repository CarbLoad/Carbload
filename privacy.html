CarbLoad Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

CarbLoad ("the app", "we") is a personal sports-nutrition tracking application. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, used solely to identify your account and let you log in.
  • Nutrition and training data you enter yourself: food, hydration, weight, micronutrient and workout logs.
  • Data from fitness/health trackers you choose to connect (such as Whoop or Strava), including recovery, strain, sleep, heart-rate and activity metrics, retrieved via each provider's official API once you authorize the connection.

How we use it

Your data is used only to power the features of the app itself: calculating nutrition targets, showing your logged history, and generating recovery/fueling guidance based on your connected trackers. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any third party except the infrastructure providers needed to run the app (see below).

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database, protected by row-level security so that only your own account can read or write your data. The app itself is hosted on Netlify.

Third-party connections

If you connect a tracker such as Whoop or Strava, we request only the data scopes needed to power the app's features (e.g. recovery, sleep, strain, activity data), using each provider's standard OAuth authorization flow. You can disconnect a tracker at any time from the app's settings, which stops any further data from being retrieved.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
